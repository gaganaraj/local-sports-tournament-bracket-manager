# Local Sports Tournament & Bracket Manager

## Lab 1 – Requirements Engineering & UML Use-Case Modelling

### Project Overview

The **Local Sports Tournament & Bracket Manager** is a system designed to simplify the organization and management of local sports tournaments.

The system helps tournament organizers manage teams, players, matches, tournament brackets, schedules, results, and notifications in a structured and efficient manner.

It reduces manual work and provides a clear way to track tournament progress from registration to completion.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of the system are:

- To manage local sports tournaments efficiently.
- To register and manage teams and players.
- To generate tournament brackets.
- To schedule and manage matches.
- To record match results.
- To automatically update tournament progression.
- To provide organizers with an organized tournament management system.
- To reduce errors associated with manual tournament management.

---

## Main Actors

The major actors identified for the system are:

### 1. Tournament Organizer

The Tournament Organizer manages the overall tournament.

Responsibilities include:

- Create and manage tournaments
- Register teams
- Manage players
- Generate tournament brackets
- Schedule matches
- Update match results
- Monitor tournament progress

### 2. Team Captain

The Team Captain represents a participating team.

Responsibilities include:

- Register the team
- Manage team information
- View match schedules
- View tournament brackets
- View match results
- Track team progress

### 3. Player

Players participate in tournaments as members of registered teams.

Responsibilities include:

- View tournament information
- View team information
- View match schedules
- View match results
- Track tournament progress

---

## Functional Requirements

The system should provide the following major functionalities:

1. Tournament creation and management
2. Team registration
3. Player management
4. Tournament bracket generation
5. Match scheduling
6. Match result management
7. Tournament progress tracking
8. Team and player information management
9. Viewing tournament brackets
10. Viewing match schedules and results
